Company Profile

A Canadian Environmental Story Over 100 Years In The Making

Founded in Toronto in 1919, B2B Laboratories continues to manufacture professional chemical products with a responsibility to safer chemistry and a refusal to settle for mediocre performance.

Safer Chemistry, No Compromise On Performance

B2B serves industrial, automotive, fleet, utility, hydro, agriculture, construction, mining, food processing, transportation, and facility maintenance customers with practical products built for real operating conditions.

Product discovery, application guidance, quote workflows, and technical document access stay close to the product record so customers can move from research to action quickly.

Operating Principles

  • Performance-focused professional chemistry
  • Environmental responsibility and safer product direction
  • Technical support for regulated industrial buyers
  • Food-processing and CFIA documentation awareness
